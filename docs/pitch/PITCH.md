# DayBridge — 2-Minute Pitch Script

Teammate B, October 2026. Timing notes in brackets. Cut anything to hit the clock — the demo is the main event.

## The statistic (0:00–0:25)
> "One in three older adults reports feeling lonely — and chronic loneliness raises the risk of dementia, heart disease, and early death as much as smoking 15 cigarettes a day. [cite: Holt-Lunstad 2015, Perspectives on Psychological Health / CDC loneliness factsheet — pick one and memorize it.]
> Here's the thing: most seniors *want* to be out doing things. The barrier isn't motivation. It's logistics."

## The product (0:25–0:45)
> "DayBridge turns 'I like music, I can't drive, I use a walker' into a real weekly plan — one that checks stairs, buses, budgets, hearing, and group size before it recommends anything. And when something doesn't work, it says exactly why, in plain words."

## Live demo — Margaret (0:45–1:40)
> "This is Margaret. She's 82, uses a walker, rides the bus, $10 a week, hard of hearing. One click."
> [Load demo: Margaret → plan appears]
> "Two things she can actually do: cards at the Central Library — step-free, hearing loop, free — and Ukulele & Singing at the North Berkeley Senior Center. Both real Berkeley programs."
> [Point at amber lines] "One soft barrier: it's a noisy room, so DayBridge suggests sitting near the leader."
> [Scroll to 'Why not these?'] "And the moment that makes the demo: the choir. Margaret loves choir. But the entrance has stairs, and she uses a walker. DayBridge doesn't pretend it'll work — it says so, and puts her somewhere she can actually get into."
> [Optional: 'Get me there' → /coordinate] "And it doesn't just suggest — it asks permission, then lines up a ride from her son and an invite to a friend, and only marks it ready when everyone confirms."

## Privacy + architecture (1:40–2:00)
> "Everything deterministic — stairs, cost, travel — is a checked rule, not a guess. The AI explanation runs **on-device via RunAnywhere**: her health and mobility data never leaves this laptop. Every claim has a source. Plans print, get saved, and get shared with family."

## Close (2:00)
> "The barrier was never motivation. It's logistics. DayBridge handles the logistics."
